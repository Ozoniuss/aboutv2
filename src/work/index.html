If we're going to work together, we should share some core values. Mine are:

  • staying curious
  • data-driven decisions
  • mutual trust
  • respecting time
  • building something meaningful
  • working with passion
  • problem-solving

Let's cover them in more detail.


We work in a field where you can't really get comfortable. Unless you stay curious, you will be left behind. There is no point where you know enough to stop learning.

I'm a data-driven person. I see software partly as an art form and understand some decisions have to be made based on feeling or taste, but when you have data available, you should use it to your advantage. Just make sure the data is reliable ;)

I find the most important trait of a high-performing team is not good processes but rather trust amongst its members in both good times and bad. We should cherish achievements together but also understand that we make mistakes. Failure is the best learning opportunity.

I value my time and I'm sure you value yours. I'm obsessed with using my time in the most efficient way possible. Being blocked for 30 minutes once in a while can happen. Being blocked regularly on things and not doing anything about it is a choice.

I care about working on software that is meaningful. The journey of building will always be fun, but nothing replaces the feeling you get when you see your work being useful to someone. Even if that someone is just you.

I like working with people who are passionate about what they do. It just gives a certain kind of energy that clearly shows in the output.

I have always been a problem solver. Just look at my GitHub if you don't believe it. To me, solving problems is what software engineering is all about. At the same time, I think we should remind ourselves what the end goal is before we get attached to our own solutions.


If this list resonates with you or your team, then we may be a good fit to work together. Hit me up at alexxbledea@gmail.com if you're interested in a collaboration.
